# 🚗 Kaskasero!

A Filipino-themed 2D highway racing game that runs right in your browser. Weave through jeepneys, tricycles and trucks on road trips across the whole Philippines: **Luzon**, **Visayas** and **Mindanao**!

## ▶ Play now

### 👉 [**Play Kaskasero!**](https://ysthangabrielcolarina-lang.github.io/kaskasero/)

```
https://ysthangabrielcolarina-lang.github.io/kaskasero/
```

Works on computers and phones. No download needed.

## 🎮 How to play

| Action | Computer | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Change lanes | ← → or A / D | Swipe left / right, or tap ◀ ▶ |
| Pause | P | ❚❚ button |
| Sound effects on/off | M | 🔊 button |
| Music on/off | B | 🎵 button |
| Start | Enter / Space, then pick a map | PLAY button, then pick a map |

- **Pass vehicles** for points and grab **₱ coins** ("AYOS!").
- **Dodge at the last second** for a close call ("GALING!"). Chain them for a combo ("ASTIG!", "MUNTIK NA!").
- **One crash and it's over** ("WASAK!"), unless you have a shield.

## ⭐ Features

- **3 maps, each with its own look** (a new place every 2.5 km):
  - 🌋 **Luzon** (mountains and cities): Calauan → Bay → Los Baños → Calamba → SLEX → Tagaytay → Manila → Pampanga → Baguio → Ilocos
  - 🏝️ **Visayas** (islands and beaches): Cebu City → Bohol → Iloilo → Bacolod → Boracay
  - 🦅 **Mindanao** (rainforest and volcanoes): Cagayan de Oro → Bukidnon → Davao → General Santos → Zamboanga
- **Map screen:** before each trip you pick a map, and every card shows its whole route. Then a plane flies you over the islands to your first stop ("Lipad papuntang VISAYAS!").
- **Skyways and airplanes:** raised skyways (with traffic on top) cross over the road on SLEX, NLEX and in big cities, and planes take off over Manila, Cebu City and Davao.
- **Local landmarks:** the Pineapple Plaza and PUP Calauan, Bay's garden shops and monay bakeries, the Rizal Shrine in Calamba, plus Mt. Kalisungan, Mt. Makiling and Taal Volcano in the background. North of Manila: giant parols, sisig and Mt. Arayat in Pampanga; the pink cathedral, Burnham Park and strawberry farms in foggy, pine-covered Baguio; Vigan's heritage houses, Paoay Church and the Bangui windmills in Ilocos.
- **Visayas landmarks:** Magellan's Cross, the Basilica del Santo Niño and lechon in Cebu; the Chocolate Hills, a giant tarsier, the Loboc River and Baclayon Church in Bohol; Miagao Church and La Paz batchoy in Iloilo; MassKara masks, The Ruins and chicken inasal in Bacolod; paraw sailboats, Willy's Rock and beach bars in Boracay.
- **Mindanao landmarks:** whitewater rafting and ziplines in Cagayan de Oro; pineapple fields, a cattle ranch and the pyramid-shaped monastery in Bukidnon; Mt. Apo, the Philippine Eagle and durian in Davao; the giant tuna and fish port in General Santos (with a TAG-INIT challenge); Fort Pilar and colorful vintas in Zamboanga.
- **Filipino traffic:** jeepneys, tricycles, e-bikes, kalesas, provincial buses, L-300s and trucks.
- **Road events:** "PARA!" jeepney stops, cars changing lanes, and floods in Los Baños.
- **Weather and time of day:** rain, sunset and night driving with glowing tail lights.
- **Power-ups:**
  - 🛡 **Shield:** blocks one crash.
  - 🧲 **Magnet:** pulls coins to you.
  - ⏱ **Slow-mo:** traffic moves at half speed.
  - 👮 **Enforcer:** a traffic enforcer blows the whistle ("PRRRT! TABI!") and clears every vehicle off the road for 2 seconds.
- **Garage:** choose a Sports Car, Electric Car, Retro Muscle or Jeepney. Your pick spins 360° on a turntable in the Kaskasero Garage (drag it to turn it yourself).
- **Picture guide:** the How to Play screen shows a small moving demo for every rule, power-up and danger.
- **Secret vehicle:** unlock 👑 **Hari ng Daan**, a maxed-out e-bike, by buying it for ₱2000 Pera (the pesos you collect while driving).
- **Upgrades:** spend Pera in the garage to upgrade each vehicle's Speed (+5 km/h per level) and Slim (6% narrower per level), up to level 5.
- **Paint shop:** every paint has two colors and a design (twin stripes, racing stripe, two-tone, flames, checkers, lightning, or the Watawat flag). Candy Red is free; the other 10 cost ₱40–₱150. Try one on in the garage before you buy it.
- **Sounds:** engine, horns, coins, rain and crashes, all made in code with the Web Audio API (no sound files).

## 🛠 Built with

- **HTML** for the page and menus
- **CSS** for the jeepney-art styling
- **JavaScript (Canvas 2D)** for the graphics, game logic and sounds

No libraries, no images: everything is drawn with code.

## 📁 Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page: game screen, menus and buttons |
| `style.css` | How the page looks |
| `script.js` | The whole game: road, vehicles, landmarks, rules and sounds |

To play offline, download the three files into one folder and open `index.html` in a browser.
